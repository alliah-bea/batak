Download BATAK, our Batak - English translator.

**System Requirements**

Device: Android mobile phone
Operating System: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher
RAM: At least 6.0 GB
Storage: At least 400 MB of free storage
Internet Connection: Not required except for APK download

**Launching the App**

1. Scan the QR code. The APK download should start automatically.
If the download does not start or gets stuck on “Download Pending”: open the page in a browser, then copy and paste the page link in the address bar — or type it manually: https://tinyurl.com/batak-app
2. Install the app. (If prompted, allow installation from unknown sources.)
3. Once installed, tap on the BATAK app icon to launch the app.

**Navigating the Interface**

1. Use the navigation bar found at the bottom to switch between screens.
2. To navigate to the Translation screen, tap the Translation (left) button.
3. To navigate to the Dictionary screen, tap the Dictionary (middle) button.
4. To navigate to the Settings screen, tap the Settings (right) button.

**Performing a Translation**

1. Navigate to the Translation screen.
2. Wait for the translator to load.
3. In the upper box, enter the word, phrase, or simple sentence you wish to translate.
4. Tap the “Translate” button.
5. The translated text will be displayed in the lower box.
6. To switch translation direction (from Batak–English to English–Batak or vice versa), tap the “Swap” button.
7. To clear text from both boxes, tap the “Clear” button.

**Using the Dictionary**

1. Navigate to the Dictionary screen.
2. Scroll to navigate the entries of the dictionary as needed.
3. To quickly browse entries by their first letter, tap any letter on the sidebar.
4. To sort entries from A–Z or Z–A, use the sort drop-down found in the upper right.
5. To look for a specific word, type it in the search bar found at the top.

**Adjusting Settings**

1. Navigate to the Settings screen.
2. To change the app’s accent color, choose a color from the color wheel.
3. To change the app’s font style, click the font drop-down, choose a font, then click the “Apply Font” button.
4. To change the app’s text size, adjust the slider to the desired size.
